Community technology nonprofit

Serving Ohio communities one piece of hardware at a time.

2ndBoot is a nonprofit organization made by high school students for the greater Ohio community. We collect unused computers and cyberwaste, and distribute them to refurbishers and community/non profit groups to help those in need.

Our mission

Why cyberwaste?

We believe in the mission that nothing should go to waste, especially when it comes to technology that can make a real difference in people's lives. That could mean your grandmother's computer from 1993, or a laptop that's been sitting in a drawer for years. Anything makes a difference.

Why we exist

Technology, any technology, shouldn't go to waste at all.

Countless companies throw or shred their old computers, hard drives, monitors, and other hardware even though there are ways for it to have a second life.

  • Collect

    We accept any kind of cyberwaste, whether it's a single hard drive or an entire desktop computer. Just shoot us a email and we'll arrange plans.

  • Redistribute

    We work with community organizations to ensure devices reach those who need them most.

  • Place

    Finished machines go to people referred by libraries, shelters, schools, and partner nonprofits.
