Why we exist

A working computer should not be a luxury.

Homework, job applications, telehealth, and small-business bookkeeping all assume a device and a connection. Plenty of usable hardware is sitting in closets while neighbors go without.

  • Collect

    We take laptops, desktops, monitors, and accessories from households, offices, and schools.

  • Refurbish

    Volunteers test hardware, replace parts when we can, and securely erase old data.

  • Place

    Finished machines go to people referred by libraries, shelters, schools, and partner nonprofits.

What we do

Programs built around reuse, not landfill.

Device donation

Drop off a working or repairable computer. We handle data wipe and keep what can still serve someone else.

Community workshop

Volunteers learn basic hardware repair while preparing machines for distribution days.

Digital starter kits

Recipients get a ready-to-use laptop, a simple getting-started guide, and a path to local digital-skills help.

Get involved

Give hardware, time, or a gift that keeps a machine in service.

Donate a device

Laptops from the last 8–10 years, chargers, and 8GB+ RAM machines help most. Remove the password if you can.

Volunteer

Intake, testing, OS install, and distribution days all need people. No repair background required.

Fund parts

SSDs, RAM, and batteries turn almost-good computers into daily drivers. Ask us for the current parts list.
